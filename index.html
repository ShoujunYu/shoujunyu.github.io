About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Pattern Recognition and Intelligent Systems at the Shenzhen Institutes of Advanced Technology (SIAT), Chinese Academy of Sciences.

My research interests include diffusion MRI, brain microstructure imaging, brain fiber tractography, and artificial intelligence for medical imaging and reconstruction.

Email: sj.yu1@siat.ac.cn GitHub: github.com/ShoujunYu ORCID: 0000-0001-9865-8190
