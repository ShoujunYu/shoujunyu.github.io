This study investigates the effects of gamma-type immunoglobulin (IgG) on microglial responses to amyloid-beta pathology. It shows that IgG accumulates on microglia in mouse models of amyloid pathology and enhances microglial phagocytosis of amyloid-beta fibrils via its Fc fragment, suggesting a possible link between peripheral immunity and microglial function in Alzheimer's disease.
