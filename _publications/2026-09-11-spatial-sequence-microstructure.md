A spatial–sequence joint learning framework for accelerated multi-model diffusion MRI microstructure estimation from sparsely sampled q-space data. It uses a shared encoder combining CNN-based spatial features with Mamba-based sequence features and model-specific decoders. Experiments on HCP, ADNI, and Tiantan clinical datasets demonstrate robust multi-model parameter estimation across a range of q-space sampling factors.
