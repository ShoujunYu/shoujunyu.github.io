I am a Ph.D. student in Pattern Recognition and Intelligent Systems at the Shenzhen Institutes of Advanced Technology (SIAT), Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS).

My research focuses on **medical image analysis, magnetic resonance imaging (MRI), and artificial intelligence**, with particular interests in diffusion MRI, brain microstructure imaging, and deep learning-based medical image reconstruction.

My current work focuses on developing computational methods for diffusion MRI analysis, neural fiber tractography, and fine-scale neuroanatomical imaging. I am also interested in applying artificial intelligence to improve medical imaging workflows and clinical decision-making.

## Research Interests

- **Medical Image Analysis:** Deep learning and computational methods for medical imaging.
- **Diffusion MRI:** Diffusion signal reconstruction, microstructural modeling, and quantitative analysis.
- **Brain Tractography:** Deep learning-based fiber tracking and cranial nerve visualization.
- **MRI Reconstruction:** Advanced reconstruction and image enhancement techniques.
- **Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare:** AI-assisted diagnosis and medical imaging workflows.

## Research

My research primarily focuses on the following areas:

**Diffusion MRI and Brain Microstructure Imaging**

Developing computational methods to improve diffusion MRI reconstruction, microstructural characterization, and the representation of complex neural structures.

**Deep Learning for Fiber Tractography**

Investigating deep learning-based approaches for reconstructing neural fiber trajectories from diffusion MRI, with applications to brain connectivity analysis and cranial nerve imaging.

**AI for Medical Image Analysis**

Exploring artificial intelligence methods for medical image interpretation, quantitative analysis, and clinical decision support.
