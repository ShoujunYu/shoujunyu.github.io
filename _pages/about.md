I am currently a Ph.D. student in Pattern Recognition and Intelligent Systems at the Shenzhen Institutes of Advanced Technology (SIAT), Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS).

My research focuses on **medical image analysis, magnetic resonance imaging (MRI), and artificial intelligence**, with particular interests in diffusion MRI, brain microstructure imaging, and deep learning-based medical image reconstruction.

My current work focuses on developing computational methods for diffusion MRI analysis, neural fiber tractography, and fine-scale neuroanatomical imaging. I am also interested in applying artificial intelligence to improve medical imaging workflows and clinical decision-making.


## Research Interests

Diffusion MRI and Brain Microstructure: Advanced MRI reconstruction, diffusion signal modeling, and quantitative characterization of brain microstructure.

Neural Fiber Tractography: Deep learning-based fiber tracking, brain connectivity mapping, and cranial nerve visualization.

AI for Medical Imaging: Deep learning methods for medical image analysis, computer-aided diagnosis, and clinical workflow optimization.
